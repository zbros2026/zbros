Help Center

Frequently asked questions

Find answers about ordering, delivery, exchanges, sizing, payments and bulk rates.

How do I place an order?

Use the order form or message us on WhatsApp with the items and sizes you want. We'll confirm everything before dispatch.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, we ship pan-India. Delivery time depends on your city and is confirmed with your order.

Can I exchange if the size doesn't fit?

Yes, exchanges are available on unworn items with tags intact. Message us to arrange it.

How do I find my size?

Send us your usual size or measurements on WhatsApp and we'll recommend the best fit.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, bank transfer and other standard methods. Payment details are shared when you confirm your order.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale rates?

Yes, for larger quantities message us for special pricing.
